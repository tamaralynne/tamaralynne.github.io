The Eastside Forum Project was a neighborhood-based theatre project that focused on communities living, working, and accessing services along East 82nd Avenue in Portland Oregon. With support from Regional Arts and Culture Council, Multnomah County Cultural Coalition, and SE Uplift, community members created an original interactive forum play "Lunch" exploring the experience of cast members who'd experienced homelessness, and inviting the audiences to step onto the stage and enact solutions.
