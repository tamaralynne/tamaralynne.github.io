Selected public talks, conferences, and symposia.

## "What Happened???"
Tamara Lynne with Portland Story Theater

A storytelling performance about the risks and rewards of an international visit, to research theatre and social movements in Brazil's Central West.

## Alliance of Artist Communities
Hosted by Regional Arts and Culture Council

A panel presentation with community partners and participants of the Intersections Artist Residency at Bud Clark Commons.

## Annexation and Assimilation

A residency and performance with students from Harrison Park School, in collaboration with Sabina Haque, Artist in Residence with City of Portland Archives and Records Center.

## What is a Place?
At the Intersection of Housing and Transit Justice

Theatre creation with communities living, working, and accessing services in the Jade-Midway District, as part of the APANO (Asian Pacific American Network of Oregon) Placemaking Artist Collaborative.

## Home is Where the Heart is
Pitch a Tent

Forum Theatre on the streets, as part of engaging housed and houseless neighbors in dialogue about homelessness, in collaboration with Right to Survive and with support from Oregon Humanities.
