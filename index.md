Tamara Lynne is an interdisciplinary artist, writer and theatre-maker with 20 years of socially engaged, community devised performance, in both local and international collaborations. Her work centers on artful investigation of systems and ecosystems, questions of agency, and exploration of the ways that we can collaborate to build resilience, create spaces of refuge, and collectively imagine and transform our world.
